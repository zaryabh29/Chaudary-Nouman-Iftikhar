Profile

A second-generation builder with a first-principles mandate.

Raised inside Mian Group of Chakwal, founded by his father Ch. Iftikhar in 1990 to bring international-standard home appliances to Pakistani households through installment plans.

CEO of the group since 2000. In August 2019 he founded MGC Developments to apply the same installment logic to housing, building residential, commercial and hospitality high-rises across Bahria Town and DHA, Rawalpindi/Islamabad.

Known for insisting on RDA approvals, legally clear titles and real rental value over speculative promises.

Perspective

“Real estate must be backed by real rental value. Anything else is just a speculative promise.”

  1. 01

    Legally clear before commercially attractive.

    RDA approval comes first; a project without it is not offered.

  2. 02

    Protect investor trust the way a bank protects deposits.

    Systems, compliance and presentation are held to institutional standards.

  3. 03

    Credibility over quick commissions.

    Sales built on real demand and real rental value outlast anything built on promises.
