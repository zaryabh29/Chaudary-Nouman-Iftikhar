The Visionary

Built on Legacy.
Driven by Vision.

"True success is not what you build for yourself — it is how many lives you transform along the way."

Born into the entrepreneurial spirit of Chakwal, Chaudary Nouman Iftikhar inherited more than a business — he inherited a responsibility. When his father, Ch. Iftikhar, founded Mian Group in 1990, the goal was simple: give every Pakistani family access to quality home appliances at fair prices. That promise still beats at the heart of everything Ch. Nouman builds today.

Under his leadership, Mian Electronics grew from a single store into a network of over 126 national outlets, becoming one of Pakistan's largest home appliance retailers. But Ch. Nouman's ambition extended further — into the skylines of Rawalpindi and Islamabad.

As Founder and CEO of MGC Developments, he pioneered the revolutionary 1% Payment Plan — a model that democratized luxury real estate investment across Pakistan, earning him two prestigious Presidential Awards and national recognition for economic innovation.

The Journey

A Legacy Decades in the Making

From a single shop in Chakwal to a business empire spanning electronics and luxury real estate — a story of vision, persistence, and nation-building.

1990
The Foundation Stone
Ch. Iftikhar establishes the Mian Group of Chakwal — a small electronics and home appliances business with a big promise: quality goods at honest prices for every Pakistani family.
2005 – 2015
National Expansion
Under Ch. Nouman's leadership, Mian Electronics expands aggressively across Pakistan. The 0% markup installment model wins the trust of millions, growing to 50+ outlets nationwide.
2016
MGC Developments Founded
Ch. Nouman founds MGC Developments, entering the real estate sector with an ambition to build world-class luxury properties accessible to every income bracket.
2020 – 2022
126+ Outlets & Growing
Mian Electronics reaches a network of over 126 national outlets across Pakistan, cementing its position as one of the country's most trusted retail brands for home appliances.
2022
Presidential Award — Top Taxpayer
At Aiwan-e-Sadr, Ch. Nouman Iftikhar and MGC Developments receive recognition as one of Pakistan's highest taxpayers — a testament to transparent, nation-first business ethics.
2023
Highest Accreditation Award — Pakistan Property Expo
The President of Pakistan presents MGC Developments with the Highest Accreditation Award at the Pakistan Property Expo 2023, recognizing excellence in quality, reliability, and innovation.
2023 – Present
The 1% Payment Plan Revolution
MGC Developments launches the pioneering 1% Payment Plan — 20% down payment, then just 1% monthly installments. Luxury real estate is now within reach for millions of Pakistanis. A national first.
Business Empire

Two Pillars of an Empire

Ch. Nouman Iftikhar leads two distinct but complementary ventures — each market leader in its own right.

Mian Electronics
Mian Group of Chakwal

Pakistan's trusted home appliance and electronics retail giant. Founded in 1990, Mian Electronics has grown into a national institution — delivering quality refrigerators, ACs, washing machines, LEDs, and mobile phones to millions of Pakistanis through flexible 0% markup installment plans. MGC Developments
Luxury Real Estate, Rawalpindi

Founded by Ch. Nouman Iftikhar, MGC Developments has redefined luxury real estate in Pakistan. Operating in Bahria Town Rawalpindi and the twin cities, MGC builds award-winning high-rises with world-class amenities — from RDA-approved 27-storey towers with Best Western Plus hotels to artist-inspired commercial destinations. The pioneering 1% Payment Plan has opened doors to luxury living for a generation of new investors.
